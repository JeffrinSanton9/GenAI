=======================================================
SCHOLARSHIP NOTIFICATIONS 2025-2026
=======================================================

A. Tamil Nadu Government Scholarships
-----------------------------------------
1. Post-Matric Scholarship (SC/ST)
   Eligibility: SC/ST students; family income < Rs. 2.5 lakh/year
   Amount: Full tuition fee reimbursement
   Application: Through TN e-Scholarships portal (tnscholarships.gov.in)
   Deadline: 31 October 2025

2. First Graduate Scholarship
   Eligibility: First person in the family to attend college; income < Rs. 3 lakh/year
   Amount: Rs. 5,000 per year
   Application: Through college welfare office
   Deadline: 30 September 2025

B. Central Government Scholarships
-----------------------------------------
3. Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship (CSSS)
   Eligibility: Students scoring above 80th percentile in Class 12 Board exams; family income < Rs. 8 lakh/year
   Amount: Rs. 10,000 per year (UG); Rs. 20,000 per year (PG)
   Application: National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in)
   Deadline: 31 October 2025

4. Prime Minister's Special Scholarship Scheme (PMSSS)
   Eligibility: J&K domicile students pursuing courses in institutions outside J&K
   Not applicable for most students at this institution.

C. Institution-Level Scholarships
-----------------------------------------
5. Merit Scholarship (SLCET)
   Eligibility: Students securing First Rank in their respective department in the previous semester; CGPA ≥ 9.0
   Amount: 50% tuition fee waiver for the next semester
   Application: Apply to the Principal's office with marksheet
   Deadline: 2 weeks after results declaration

6. Sports Scholarship (SLCET)
   Eligibility: Students representing Tamil Nadu at state/national level in sports
   Amount: Full tuition fee waiver for one semester
   Conditions: Must maintain 75% attendance and pass all subjects
   Application: Through sports coordinator with selection letter

7. Need-Based Financial Aid (SLCET)
   Eligibility: Students from economically weaker sections (EWS); annual family income < Rs. 1.5 lakh/year; 2nd year and above
   Amount: Up to Rs. 20,000 per year (partial fee waiver)
   Application: Apply to the Welfare Committee with income certificate and bank statement
   Deadline: 15 October 2025 (for second-year students and above)

D. How to Apply for Scholarships
-----------------------------------------
Step 1: Confirm eligibility criteria carefully.
Step 2: Collect required documents (income certificate, caste certificate if applicable, bank passbook, Aadhaar, marksheets).
Step 3: Submit application through the respective portal or college welfare office before the deadline.
Step 4: Follow up with the college welfare office for status updates.
Step 5: Scholarship disbursements are typically credited directly to the student's bank account (Aadhaar-linked).
