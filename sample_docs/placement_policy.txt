PLACEMENT AND INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES 2024-2025
Training and Placement Cell
Sri Lakshmi College of Engineering and Technology
Last Updated: August 2024

=======================================================
SECTION 1: PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
=======================================================

1.1 General Eligibility
To participate in on-campus placement drives, a final-year student must meet ALL of the following criteria:

(a) Academic Performance: Minimum CGPA of 6.0 (on a 10-point scale) as of the 7th semester.
(b) Backlogs: Zero active backlogs at the time of the placement drive. However, students with history of backlogs (now cleared) may participate with the permission of the Placement Officer. Students with active backlog in the current semester are NOT eligible.
(c) Attendance: 75% attendance in the current semester (at the time of the drive).
(d) Registration: Must have registered with the Training and Placement Cell (TPC) by 31st August of the final year.
(e) Documents: Valid college ID, up-to-date resume uploaded on the TPC portal, and two passport photographs.

1.2 Company-Specific Criteria
Individual companies may impose additional criteria such as:
- Higher CGPA threshold (e.g., 7.0 or 7.5 CGPA).
- No backlogs including historical backlogs.
- Specific branch/department restrictions.
- Aptitude/coding test cutoffs.

Students must check company-specific criteria on the TPC notice board or portal before applying.

1.3 Multiple Offers Policy
A student who has already received a placement offer (CTC ≥ Rs. 4 LPA) may participate in further drives only if explicitly permitted by the recruiting company and the TPC. Students should not withhold acceptance of an offer to gamble on higher-paying drives.

=======================================================
SECTION 2: PLACEMENT PROCESS
=======================================================

2.1 Registration and Resume
Students must register on the TPC portal (tpc.slcet.edu.in) and submit:
- Updated resume (PDF, max 2 pages)
- Semester mark sheets (scanned copies)
- 10th and 12th certificates
- Any certifications, internship letters

2.2 Pre-Placement Talks (PPT)
Companies may conduct a Pre-Placement Talk before the recruitment process. Attendance at PPT is mandatory for participating students; absence without prior intimation to TPC will disqualify the student from that drive.

2.3 Recruitment Rounds
Typical recruitment process includes:
Round 1: Aptitude Test (Quantitative, Verbal, Logical Reasoning)
Round 2: Technical Test / Coding Assessment
Round 3: Group Discussion (not all companies)
Round 4: Technical Interview
Round 5: HR Interview
Final: Offer Letter

2.4 Conduct During Recruitment
Any form of malpractice during tests, dishonesty during interviews, or sharing questions with others is grounds for immediate disqualification and may be reported to the company. The student will be barred from all subsequent placement drives.

=======================================================
SECTION 3: INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES
=======================================================

3.1 Eligibility for Internship Approval
Third-year students (5th and 6th semester) and final-year students may undertake internships. An internship must be approved by the Head of Department before commencement.

3.2 Documents Required for Internship Approval
Students must submit the following to the department office for internship approval:
1. Internship Offer Letter from the company (on company letterhead).
2. Internship Application Form (available at dept. office or college portal).
3. Consent Letter from Parent/Guardian (signed).
4. HOD Approval Form – to be signed by the Head of Department.
5. Faculty Coordinator Assignment Form – your internship supervisor.

Submission deadline: At least 10 working days before the internship start date.

3.3 Internship Duration and Credit
Internships of 4 weeks or more during the summer vacation may be eligible for academic credit (2 credits) subject to submission of:
- Daily log/diary signed by company mentor.
- Internship completion certificate.
- Final internship report (min. 30 pages) in the prescribed format.
- Presentation before the departmental committee.

3.4 Internship Stipend
The college does not guarantee stipend. Any stipend is between the student and the company. Students must not misrepresent college support for financial gain.

=======================================================
SECTION 4: SOFT SKILLS AND TRAINING
=======================================================

4.1 Mandatory Training Programs
Final-year students must complete the following TPC-conducted training programs:
- Aptitude and Reasoning (40 hours)
- Communication and Soft Skills (20 hours)
- Resume Writing and Interview Preparation (10 hours)
- Mock Interviews (compulsory; 2 rounds minimum)

Non-completion of training will result in the student being ineligible for campus placements.

4.2 Online Certifications
Students are encouraged to obtain certifications from platforms like NPTEL, Coursera, or NASSCOM. Certifications must be uploaded to the TPC portal before 31st October of the final year.
