=======================================================
CHAPTER 1: ATTENDANCE POLICY
=======================================================

1.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject (theory and practical) to be eligible to appear in the End Semester Examination (ESE). Students who fall below 75% will be barred from writing the examination and will be required to repeat the semester.

1.2 Condonation of Attendance
A student with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for attendance condonation on medical or other valid grounds. The application must be submitted to the Head of Department within 7 working days of the last date of the semester. Condonation is granted only once per academic year and requires supporting documents (medical certificate, official letter).

1.3 Medical Leave
Medical leave is counted separately from attendance. A student on medical leave for more than 10 consecutive working days must submit a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, countersigned by the college medical officer.

1.4 Attendance for Practical Classes
Attendance is compulsory for all lab/practical sessions. Missing a practical class requires written permission from the faculty in advance; otherwise it is marked absent. Attendance for practicals is calculated separately from theory.

=======================================================
CHAPTER 2: INTERNAL ASSESSMENT AND EXAMINATIONS
=======================================================

2.1 Internal Assessment Tests
Three internal assessment (CIA) tests are conducted per semester for each theory subject. The best two out of three will be considered for the internal mark calculation. Each CIA test carries 50 marks; the average of the best two is scaled to 20 marks.

2.2 Re-test Policy
A student who misses a CIA test due to a genuine reason (medical emergency, family bereavement, or official college activity) may apply for a re-test. The application must be submitted to the respective subject faculty within 3 working days of the test, along with supporting documents. Re-tests are granted at the faculty's discretion and the department head's approval. A student cannot request more than one re-test per subject per semester.

2.3 Assignment Submission
Assignments must be submitted on or before the due date specified by the faculty. Late submissions (up to 3 days) will be accepted with a 20% penalty on marks. Submissions beyond 3 days will not be accepted without prior written permission from the department head.

2.4 End Semester Examination (ESE)
The ESE is conducted by Anna University at the end of each semester. Students must register for ESE through the college portal by the notified date. ESE hall tickets are issued to eligible students (75% attendance minimum) through the department office.

2.5 Grace Marks
Anna University may award grace marks (up to 5 marks per paper) to students who have participated in sports, cultural, or NCC/NSS activities at state or national level. The student must apply through the sports/cultural coordinator with supporting certificates.

=======================================================
CHAPTER 3: CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINE
=======================================================

3.1 Code of Conduct
Students are expected to maintain decorum in classrooms, labs, library, and all college premises. Mobile phones must be switched off or in silent mode during class hours. Possession of prohibited items (alcohol, drugs, weapons) is a punishable offence leading to immediate suspension.

3.2 Disciplinary Action
Ragging in any form (physical, verbal, online) is a cognizable offence under UGC Regulations 2009. Any reported instance will result in immediate suspension and police complaint. The Anti-Ragging Committee meets monthly and maintains a helpline at 1800-180-5522.

3.3 Dress Code
Students must adhere to the college dress code: formal attire (collared shirt/salwar kameez with ID card) on all working days. Casual wear is permitted only during events specified by the college. Violation results in denial of entry.

=======================================================
CHAPTER 4: HOSTEL AND TRANSPORT
=======================================================

4.1 Hostel Admission
Hostel admission is available for out-station students on a first-come, first-served basis. Applications open in June every year. Students must submit original TC, conduct certificate, and 4 passport photos at the time of admission.

4.2 Hostel Rules
Hostel gates close at 9:00 PM on weekdays and 10:00 PM on weekends. Overnight outings require written permission from the hostel warden countersigned by a parent/guardian. Visitors are allowed only in the designated visitor's room between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM.

4.3 College Bus / Transport
Bus routes cover major areas of the city. The transport fee must be paid along with tuition fees. Students availing transport services must display their bus pass when boarding. Any change in bus route requires a written application to the transport coordinator at least 5 working days in advance.

=======================================================
CHAPTER 5: LIBRARY RULES
=======================================================

5.1 Membership and Borrowing
Every enrolled student is a member of the college library. UG students may borrow up to 3 books at a time; PG students may borrow up to 5 books. Books are issued for a loan period of 14 days (renewable once if no reservation exists).

5.2 Library Fine Structure
Overdue Fine: Rs. 2 per book per day after the due date.
Loss of Book: The student must replace the book with a new copy of the same edition OR pay 3 times the current market price of the book.
Damage to Book: Rs. 50 to Rs. 500 depending on the extent of damage, as assessed by the librarian.
Reference books and periodicals are not available for borrowing.

5.3 Library Timings
Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Closed on Sundays and public holidays.

5.4 Silence and Conduct in Library
Complete silence must be maintained. Mobile phones must be on silent mode. Food and beverages are not permitted inside the library. Violation may result in suspension of library privileges for up to one semester.
